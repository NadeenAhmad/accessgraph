Accessibility expert review

Thank you for helping

You will look at elements that a tool found in real web apps and decide, for each one, whether it is an accessibility problem under WCAG 2.2.

How it works

  • Each item asks one question, for example whether a link's text says where it goes.
  • You see the screen with the element outlined in orange and, where the user does something, a short video and the screen afterwards. The orange outlines and labels are drawn by us to show where to look; they are not part of the app.
  • Below the screens, a short list of facts shows what screen readers read for the element next to the text on the page. When a question names what screen readers read, judge that text.
  • Step 1: give your own answer, Yes, it's fine, No (violation) or Can't tell, with a short reason. Judge from the screens and the facts below them.
  • Step 2: after you save, the tool's answer and its explanation appear, and you say whether you agree. Your step 1 answer cannot be changed after that.
  • Each item takes about 1–2 minutes: … items in total. You can stop at any time and come back with the same link; your answers are kept. Please use the same computer and browser each time, not a private window.
